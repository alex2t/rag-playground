# Revenue Cloud Quote-to-Cash

Revenue Cloud Quote-to-Cash is a service from Larkspur Cloud Partners. It configures Revenue Cloud for product catalogue, pricing, quoting, approvals and renewals across the quote-to-cash process.

## Service Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Salesforce product | Revenue Cloud |
| Pricing model | Capped time and materials |
| Price | 91,600 USD |
| Typical duration | 16 weeks |
| Launch year | 2019 |
| Lead | Gideon Rasmussen |

## Deliverables

- Quote-to-cash process map
- Product catalogue with up to 200 products
- Pricing rules and discount schedules
- Quote templates and approval flows
- Contract and renewal configuration
- UAT report and go-live plan

## Scope

### Included

- Price book and bundle configuration
- Up to 8 approval paths
- Quote document generation in up to 2 templates
- Renewal quote automation
- Training for up to 20 sales operations users

### Excluded

- ERP integration
- Tax engine configuration
- Historical invoice migration

## Client Prerequisites

- Revenue Cloud licences purchased before kickoff
- Current price lists and discount policies
- A finance stakeholder available for approval rule decisions
