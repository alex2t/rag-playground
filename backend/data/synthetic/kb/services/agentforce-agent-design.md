## Overview

Agentforce Agent Design designs, configures and tests Agentforce agents with defined topics, actions and guardrails for a client's support or sales use case. The service launched in 2024.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|

## Deliverables

- Agent use-case brief
- Up to 4 configured agent topics with instructions
- Guardrail and escalation rules
- Test plan with 120 scripted conversations
- Agent performance review after launch

## Scope

### Included

- Topic and action design for up to 4 topics
- Instruction and prompt writing
- Up to 6 standard actions configured
- Sandbox testing and one tuning cycle

### Excluded

- Voice channel setup
- Custom model training
- Knowledge article authoring

## Client Prerequisites

- Agentforce capacity purchased
- Knowledge articles approved by the client
- A subject-matter expert available 4 hours per week
