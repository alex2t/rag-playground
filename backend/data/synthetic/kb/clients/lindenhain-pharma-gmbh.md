## Projects

### Lindenhain Wholesaler Quote-to-Cash

- **Service:** Revenue Cloud Quote-to-Cash
- **Dates:** 2020-01-20 to 2020-05-25
- **Team:** Gideon Rasmussen, Calista Brandt, Rowan Marlowe

**Summary:** Configured Revenue Cloud for Lindenhain's wholesaler and hospital pharmacy pricing, covering the product catalogue, contract pricing, approvals and renewals. The project ended with a UAT report and go-live plan.

**Objectives:**
- Load a catalogue with contract price lists
- Build approval flows for discount levels
- Train sales operations users on quoting

**Outcome:** Quote-to-cash went live for the commercial operations team after the UAT report was signed off.

**Metrics:**
- Catalogue of 142 products configured
- 7 approval paths built
- Quote approval time fell from 5.0 days to 1.5 days, a 70.0% reduction

### Lindenhain Security Review

- **Service:** Shield Security and Compliance Review
- **Dates:** 2021-02-22 to 2021-04-05
- **Team:** Wilhelmina Oyelaran, Idris Venkataram

**Summary:** Reviewed Lindenhain's Salesforce orgs against Shield capabilities to support a customer data protection review. The team delivered a rated findings report, Event Monitoring dashboards and a Field Audit Trail policy.

**Objectives:**
- Rate security risks across the orgs under review
- Define a Field Audit Trail policy for sensitive objects
- Design Platform Encryption for personal data fields

**Outcome:** The remediation roadmap was presented to Lindenhain's IT security team on the project end date.

**Metrics:**
- 2 orgs reviewed
- 34 findings logged, 7 rated high risk
- Platform Encryption designed for 19 fields
