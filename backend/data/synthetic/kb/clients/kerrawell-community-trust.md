## Projects

### Kerrawell Donor Journeys

- **Service:** Marketing Cloud Journey Setup
- **Start date:** 2022-10-03
- **End date:** 2022-11-28
- **Team:** Matthias Eklund, Aoife Brannigan

**Summary:** Set up Marketing Cloud for Kerrawell's fundraising team with automated welcome, giving and lapsed-donor journeys. Supporters were segmented by giving pattern and consent was documented on the subscriber list.

**Objectives:**
- Build 3 donor journeys in Journey Builder
- Segment supporters by giving pattern
- Configure sender authentication and unsubscribe handling

**Outcome:** The journeys were live for the end-of-year appeal and tracking reports were handed to the fundraising team.

**Metrics:**
- Monthly-giving sign-ups from email rose from 85 to 132 per month, a 55.3% increase
- 3 journeys configured
- 4 audience segments created

### Kerrawell Volunteer Portal

- **Service:** Experience Cloud Portal Build
- **Start date:** 2023-05-15
- **End date:** 2023-08-28
- **Team:** Saoirse Kettleby, Everett Nakamura-Boyle, Rafferty Okeke

**Summary:** Built an Experience Cloud portal where volunteers register, view shifts and log hours. The build included self-registration, a sharing model and Flow-driven forms.

**Objectives:**
- Let volunteers register and manage their shifts online
- Log volunteer hours through Flow-driven forms
- Pass an accessibility review against WCAG 2.1 AA

**Outcome:** The portal launched to volunteers on the project end date with an admin guide handed to programme staff.

**Metrics:**
- Volunteer onboarding time fell from 12 days to 5 days, a 58.3% reduction
- 2 portal user types configured
- 5 Flow-driven forms built
