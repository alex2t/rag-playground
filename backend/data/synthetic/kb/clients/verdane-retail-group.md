## Projects

### Verdane Lifecycle Email Journeys

- **Service:** Marketing Cloud Journey Setup
- **Dates:** 2019-04-08 to 2019-06-07
- **Team:** Matthias Eklund, Aoife Brannigan

**Summary:** Marketing Cloud was configured with abandoned-basket, welcome and post-purchase journeys for Verdane's online shop. Sending domain authentication and four audience segments were set up.

**Objectives:**
- Recover abandoned baskets with automated emails
- Improve email engagement with segmentation

**Outcome:** Three automated journeys run continuously, and the marketing team tracks engagement in Marketing Cloud.

**Metrics:**
- 3 journeys and 6 email templates delivered
- 4 audience segments configured
- Email click-through rate improved from 2.8% to 4.1%, a 46.4% increase

### Verdane Franchise Partner Portal

- **Service:** Experience Cloud Portal Build
- **Dates:** 2023-01-09 to 2023-04-14
- **Team:** Saoirse Kettleby, Brid Calloway, Aoife Brannigan

**Summary:** An Experience Cloud portal was built for independent franchise stores to check orders and raise support cases. Franchise owners and store managers have separate profiles and visibility.

**Objectives:**
- Give franchise stores self-service access to order status
- Cut support emails to the service team

**Outcome:** Franchise stores log support cases in the portal instead of emailing the service team.

**Metrics:**
- 2 portal user types and 7 page templates delivered
- 4 Flow-driven forms built
- Support emails reduced from 2,300 to 1,450 per month, a 37.0% reduction

### Verdane Store Support Agent

- **Service:** Agentforce Agent Design
- **Dates:** 2025-03-03 to 2025-05-02
- **Team:** Odalys Fenwick, Julien Archambault, Matthias Eklund

**Summary:** An Agentforce agent was designed for the franchise portal to answer order status and returns questions. Four topics, six standard actions and escalation rules were configured and tested.

**Objectives:**
- Answer store staff questions on orders and returns
- Escalate unresolved questions to the service team

**Outcome:** The agent launched in the portal after one tuning cycle in the sandbox.

**Metrics:**
- 4 agent topics and 6 standard actions configured
- 105 of 120 scripted conversations passed, an 87.5% pass rate
