## Projects

### Kestrelmoor Order Integration

- **Service:** MuleSoft Integration Blueprint
- **Start date:** 2018-03-05
- **End date:** 2018-05-18
- **Team:** Lorcan Mulvihill, Idris Venkataram
- **Summary:** An API-led integration layer connected Salesforce with Kestrelmoor's ERP, product master data system and shipping platform. Six integration flows were built with error handling and a runbook.
- **Objectives:**
  - Show live order status inside Salesforce
  - Remove manual updates between ERP and CRM
- **Outcome:** Sales and service teams see ERP order status in Salesforce, and the IT team operates the layer using the runbook.
- **Metrics:**
  - 6 integration flows implemented
  - 3 external systems connected
  - Order status sync delay reduced from 6 hours to 15 minutes, a 95.8% reduction

### Kestrelmoor Complaint Handling Rollout

- **Service:** Service Cloud Rollout
- **Start date:** 2022-01-10
- **End date:** 2022-03-18
- **Team:** Tobias Wainwright, Aoife Brannigan, Rafferty Okeke
- **Summary:** Service Cloud was rolled out to handle hospital customer complaints and device support requests. Four queues, console layouts for three agent profiles and a knowledge base were configured.
- **Objectives:**
  - Log every customer complaint as a tracked case
  - Shorten the initial response time
- **Outcome:** The service team resolves complaints in Service Cloud, with hypercare completed inside the ticket allowance.
- **Metrics:**
  - 4 case queues configured
  - 138 knowledge articles migrated
  - Initial response time reduced from 9.0 hours to 3.5 hours, a 61.1% reduction
  - Hypercare tickets peaked at 49 in a single month

### Kestrelmoor Quote-to-Cash

- **Service:** Revenue Cloud Quote-to-Cash
- **Start date:** 2023-09-04
- **End date:** 2024-01-05
- **Team:** Gideon Rasmussen, Calista Brandt, Dashiell Pruitt
- **Summary:** Revenue Cloud was configured for Kestrelmoor's product catalogue, tiered hospital pricing and approvals. Quote templates were produced in German and English, and renewal quotes were automated.
- **Objectives:**
  - Standardise quoting for hospital tenders
  - Speed up approvals on discounted deals
- **Outcome:** Sales operations issue bilingual quotes from Revenue Cloud with approvals routed automatically.
- **Metrics:**
  - 176 products loaded into the catalogue
  - 7 approval paths and 2 quote templates delivered
  - Quote cycle time reduced from 5.6 days to 3.1 days, a 44.6% reduction
  - 18 sales operations users trained
