## Projects

### Corvane Sales Cloud Implementation

- **Service:** Sales Cloud Implementation
- **Start date:** 2015-03-02
- **End date:** 2015-06-05
- **Team:** Calista Brandt, Dashiell Pruitt, Rowan Marlowe

**Summary:** Corvane moved its field sales team from spreadsheets to Sales Cloud. The project defined a regional lead-to-opportunity process and built pipeline dashboards for sales managers.

**Objectives:**
- Standardise the lead-to-opportunity process across regions
- Give reps and managers a single pipeline view

**Outcome:** The sales team went live on Sales Cloud with regional lead routing and role-based training completed.

**Metrics:**
- 34 users onboarded with role-based profiles
- Lead response time reduced from 26 hours to 9 hours, a 65.4% reduction
- 38,200 lead, account and contact records migrated

### Corvane Quote-to-Cash Configuration

- **Service:** Revenue Cloud Quote-to-Cash
- **Start date:** 2020-09-14
- **End date:** 2021-01-22
- **Team:** Gideon Rasmussen, Calista Brandt, Dashiell Pruitt

**Summary:** Revenue Cloud was configured to handle Corvane's product catalogue, tiered pricing and discount approvals. Quote templates and renewal quote automation were added for the contract-supply business.

**Objectives:**
- Replace manual spreadsheet quoting with governed pricing rules
- Route discount approvals by deal size

**Outcome:** Sales operations now issue quotes from Revenue Cloud using approved price books and approval paths.

**Metrics:**
- 148 products loaded into the catalogue
- 5 approval paths configured
- Average quote turnaround reduced from 3.5 days to 1.2 days, a 65.7% reduction

### Corvane ERP Integration Layer

- **Service:** MuleSoft Integration Blueprint
- **Start date:** 2021-05-10
- **End date:** 2021-07-16
- **Team:** Lorcan Mulvihill, Idris Venkataram, Calista Brandt

**Summary:** An API-led integration layer was built on MuleSoft to connect Salesforce with Corvane's ERP, warehouse management system and carrier rate service. The work included error handling, retry design and a runbook for the client IT team.

**Objectives:**
- Synchronise orders and inventory between Salesforce and the ERP
- Remove manual re-keying of order data

**Outcome:** Order and inventory data flows automatically between Salesforce and back-office systems, and the IT team received a runbook and monitoring dashboard.

**Metrics:**
- 5 integration flows implemented
- 3 external systems connected
- Order status sync delay reduced from 45 minutes to 4 minutes, a 91.1% reduction
