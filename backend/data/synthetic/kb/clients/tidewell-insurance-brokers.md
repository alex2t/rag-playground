## Projects

### Tidewell Service Cloud Rollout

- **Service:** Service Cloud Rollout
- **Dates:** 2016-09-12 to 2016-12-09
- **Team:** Tobias Wainwright, Aoife Brannigan, Dashiell Pruitt

**Summary:** Tidewell adopted Service Cloud for its policyholder support desk, with queues for claims, renewals, billing and general enquiries. Email-to-case and web-to-case replaced a shared mailbox, and a knowledge base was loaded for agents.

**Objectives:**
- Replace the shared mailbox with tracked cases
- Improve first-contact resolution on routine enquiries

**Outcome:** The support desk handles all inbound cases in Service Cloud, and hypercare ended within the agreed allowance.

**Metrics:**
- 4 case queues with assignment rules
- 112 knowledge articles migrated
- First-contact resolution improved from 54.0% to 69.0%, an increase of 15.0 percentage points
- Hypercare tickets peaked at 47 in a single month

### Tidewell Policyholder Portal

- **Service:** Experience Cloud Portal Build
- **Dates:** 2019-02-04 to 2019-05-17
- **Team:** Saoirse Kettleby, Aoife Brannigan, Dashiell Pruitt

**Summary:** A branded Experience Cloud portal was built so policyholders, brokers and loss adjusters can log in and track claims. Self-service cases and Flow-driven forms let users submit documents and ask questions online.

**Objectives:**
- Let policyholders check claim status without phoning the office
- Give brokers and loss adjusters secure access to their own cases

**Outcome:** The portal launched with three user types and a sharing model that limits each user to their own claims.

**Metrics:**
- 3 portal user types configured
- 7 page templates delivered
- Phone calls about claim status reduced from 1,420 to 910 per month, a 35.9% reduction

### Tidewell Shield Security Review

- **Service:** Shield Security and Compliance Review
- **Dates:** 2021-03-08 to 2021-04-16
- **Team:** Wilhelmina Oyelaran, Idris Venkataram

**Summary:** Two Tidewell Salesforce orgs were reviewed against Shield capabilities, with 30 days of event logs analysed. The review produced a rated findings report, a Platform Encryption design and a remediation roadmap.

**Objectives:**
- Identify gaps in access control and data protection
- Define encryption and audit policies for sensitive claim data

**Outcome:** Tidewell received a rated findings report and a remediation roadmap agreed with its compliance manager.

**Metrics:**
- 2 orgs reviewed
- 23 findings rated, 5 of them rated high
- Platform Encryption designed for 14 fields and Field Audit Trail set up on 8 objects

### Tidewell Claims Assistant Agent

- **Service:** Agentforce Agent Design
- **Dates:** 2025-02-03 to 2025-04-04
- **Team:** Odalys Fenwick, Julien Archambault, Aoife Brannigan

**Summary:** An Agentforce agent was designed to answer policy-change and claim-status questions for the customer portal. Guardrails and escalation rules route complex claims to human handlers.

**Objectives:**
- Deflect routine claim-status questions from the support desk
- Escalate sensitive claim conversations safely

**Outcome:** The agent was tuned once in the sandbox and launched with escalation to the claims queue.

**Metrics:**
- 3 agent topics and 5 standard actions configured
- 109 of 120 scripted test conversations passed, a 90.8% pass rate
