## Projects

### Brackenfield Subscriber Journeys

- **Service:** Marketing Cloud Journey Setup
- **Start date:** 2019-09-09
- **End date:** 2019-11-01
- **Team:** Matthias Eklund, Aoife Brannigan

**Summary:** Marketing Cloud was set up with a verified sending domain and three automated journeys for welcome, plan upgrade and win-back. Subscribers were split into four segments with tracking reports on opens and clicks.

**Objectives:**
- Automate lifecycle emails for new and existing subscribers
- Improve email engagement through segmentation

**Outcome:** Three journeys run automatically, and the marketing team reports on engagement from Marketing Cloud.

**Metrics:**
- 3 journeys and 5 email templates delivered
- 4 audience segments configured
- Email open rate improved from 18.4% to 27.9%, an increase of 9.5 percentage points

### Brackenfield Customer Care Rollout

- **Service:** Service Cloud Rollout
- **Start date:** 2021-02-01
- **End date:** 2021-05-07
- **Team:** Tobias Wainwright, Aoife Brannigan, Dashiell Pruitt

**Summary:** Service Cloud replaced a legacy help desk for the customer care team. Omni-Channel routing was configured for email and web channels, with console layouts for three agent profiles.

**Objectives:**
- Move all customer care cases to Service Cloud
- Clear the open case backlog

**Outcome:** Customer care closed down the legacy help desk and ran hypercare within the ticket allowance.

**Metrics:**
- Omni-Channel routing for 2 channels
- 3 agent console profiles configured
- Open cases reduced from 1,980 to 640 within 30 days of go-live, a 67.7% reduction
- Hypercare tickets peaked at 55 in a single month

### Brackenfield Billing Assistant Agent

- **Service:** Agentforce Agent Design
- **Start date:** 2024-11-04
- **End date:** 2025-01-10
- **Team:** Odalys Fenwick, Julien Archambault

**Summary:** An Agentforce agent was designed for billing questions and plan-change information, with four topics and six standard actions. A single tuning cycle was completed in the sandbox before launch.

**Objectives:**
- Answer routine billing questions automatically
- Hand over disputes to human agents with full context

**Outcome:** The agent launched with an escalation rule that routes disputes to the billing queue in Service Cloud.

**Metrics:**
- 4 agent topics and 6 standard actions configured
- 111 of 120 scripted conversations passed, a 92.5% pass rate
