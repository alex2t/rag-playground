## Projects

### Pellinore Support Desk Rollout

- **Service:** Service Cloud Rollout
- **Start date:** 2020-09-14
- **End date:** 2020-12-07
- **Team:** Tobias Wainwright, Everett Nakamura-Boyle, Dashiell Pruitt

**Summary:** Rolled out Service Cloud for Pellinore's customer support desk, replacing a shared mailbox and a spreadsheet tracker. The project set up case queues, Omni-Channel routing for email and web chat, and a knowledge base of shipment FAQs.

**Objectives**
- Replace shared-mailbox case tracking with Service Cloud
- Route cases to 4 queues by shipment issue type
- Move shipment FAQ content into a searchable knowledge base

**Outcome:** The support desk went live in October 2020 and hypercare closed on the project end date with ticket volumes inside the monthly allowance.

**Metrics**
- Average case response time fell from 7.5 hours to 3.0 hours, a 60.0% reduction
- Hypercare averaged 48 support tickets per month
- 132 knowledge articles migrated
