## Projects

### Quillon Sales Cloud Implementation

- **Service:** Sales Cloud Implementation
- **Start date:** 2020-06-08
- **End date:** 2020-08-21
- **Team:** Keiko Lindqvist, Calista Brandt, Everett Nakamura-Boyle

**Summary:** Sales Cloud was configured for Quillon's inside sales team with three process variants for new business, renewal and partner deals. Pipeline reports and forecast dashboards were delivered with rep training.

**Objectives:**
- Separate new business, renewal and partner pipelines
- Improve forecast reliability

**Outcome:** The sales team forecasts from Sales Cloud dashboards using the three process variants.

**Metrics:**
- 28 users onboarded
- 9 standard reports and 3 dashboards delivered
- Forecast variance narrowed from 22.0% to 9.0%, an improvement of 13.0 percentage points
