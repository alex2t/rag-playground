## Projects

### Ashgrove Customer Service Rollout

- **Service:** Service Cloud Rollout
- **Start date:** 2015-09-14
- **End date:** 2015-12-04
- **Team:** Tobias Wainwright, Dashiell Pruitt

**Summary:** Service Cloud was rolled out to the customer service team with queues for outages, billing, new connections and meters. Email and web channels were routed through Omni-Channel.

**Objectives:**
- Track every outage and billing enquiry as a case
- Reduce the time taken to resolve billing cases

**Outcome:** The service team works all enquiries in Service Cloud, and hypercare closed within the ticket allowance.

**Metrics:**
- 4 case queues configured
- 121 knowledge articles migrated
- Average billing case resolution time reduced from 4.8 days to 2.9 days, a 39.6% reduction
- Hypercare tickets peaked at 57 in a single month

### Ashgrove Customer Portal

- **Service:** Experience Cloud Portal Build
- **Start date:** 2019-09-23
- **End date:** 2020-01-10
- **Team:** Saoirse Kettleby, Tobias Wainwright, Dashiell Pruitt

**Summary:** An Experience Cloud portal was built for residential and commercial customers to report outages and view billing cases. Three Flow-driven forms replaced paper dispute forms.

**Objectives:**
- Offer self-service outage reporting and billing enquiries
- Reduce telephone service requests

**Outcome:** Residential and commercial customers use the portal to raise and track service requests.

**Metrics:**
- 2 portal user types and 6 page templates delivered
- Portal registrations reached 12,300 within 60 days of launch
- Phone-based service requests reduced from 9,800 to 6,900 per month, a 29.6% reduction

### Ashgrove Shield Security Review

- **End date:** 2023-03-24
