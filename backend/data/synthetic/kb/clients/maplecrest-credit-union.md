## Projects

### Maplecrest Member Service Rollout

- **Service:** Service Cloud Rollout
- **Dates:** 2019-10-07 to 2019-12-27
- **Team:** Everett Nakamura-Boyle, Tobias Wainwright, Dashiell Pruitt

**Summary:** Service Cloud was rolled out to the member contact centre with queues for cards, loans, deposits and general enquiries. Knowledge articles and Omni-Channel routing for email and web chat were configured.

**Objectives:**
- Route member enquiries to the right team automatically
- Shorten case handling time

**Outcome:** The contact centre went live on Service Cloud and completed hypercare within the allowance.

**Metrics:**
- 4 case queues configured
- 96 knowledge articles migrated
- Average case handling time reduced from 15.2 minutes to 12.1 minutes, a 20.4% reduction
- Hypercare tickets peaked at 52 in a single month

### Maplecrest Member Data Foundation

- **Service:** Data Cloud Foundations
- **Dates:** 2022-09-19 to 2022-12-02
- **Team:** Naveen Castellanos, Thandiwe Moyo-Sinclair, Everett Nakamura-Boyle

**Summary:** Data Cloud was set up to unify member data from core banking, card transactions, web analytics and the CRM. An identity resolution ruleset and three activated segments support targeted member outreach.

**Objectives:**
- Create a unified member profile
- Reduce duplicate member profiles across sources

**Outcome:** A unified member profile is live in Data Cloud, with three segments activated to one destination.

**Metrics:**
- 4 data streams configured
- 3 segments activated
- Duplicate member profiles reduced from 61,400 to 7,900, an 87.1% reduction

### Maplecrest Shield Review

- **Service:** Shield Security and Compliance Review
- **Dates:** 2023-05-08 to 2023-06-16
- **Team:** Wilhelmina Oyelaran, Idris Venkataram

**Summary:** Three Maplecrest orgs were reviewed against Shield capabilities, including 30 days of event log analysis. The work included an encryption design and Field Audit Trail setup on key objects.

**Objectives:**
- Assess protection of member financial data in Salesforce
- Define a prioritised remediation roadmap

**Outcome:** The security team accepted the findings report and began work on the remediation roadmap.

**Metrics:**
- 3 orgs reviewed
- 17 findings rated, 4 of them rated high
- Platform Encryption designed for 18 fields and Field Audit Trail set up on 9 objects

### Maplecrest Member Assistant Agent

- **Service:** Agentforce Agent Design
- **Dates:** 2024-09-09 to 2024-10-25
- **Team:** Odalys Fenwick, Julien Archambault

**Summary:** An Agentforce agent was designed with four topics covering card disputes, branch hours, loan enquiries and account alerts. Escalation rules pass identity-sensitive requests to human agents.

**Objectives:**
- Answer routine member questions without agent involvement
- Escalate identity-sensitive requests to staff

**Outcome:** The agent was tested in the sandbox, tuned once and launched with escalation to the contact centre queue.

**Metrics:**
- 4 agent topics and 6 standard actions configured
- 114 of 120 scripted conversations passed, a 95.0% pass rate
