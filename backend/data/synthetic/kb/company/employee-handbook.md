# Larkspur Cloud Partners employee handbook

This handbook sets out the policies that apply to employees of Larkspur Cloud Partners.

## Expenses

- The daily meal allowance while travelling is USD 70 for domestic trips and USD 95 for international trips.
- When a client provides a meal, the daily meal allowance is reduced by USD 20 for each meal provided; reductions for several meals on the same day combine.
- Any expense above USD 25 needs an itemised receipt submitted within 30 days of the purchase.
- Any single expense above USD 500 needs written approval from the employee's manager before it is incurred.
- Client entertainment is capped at USD 120 per person per event and needs director approval; a meal charged as client entertainment cannot also be claimed under the daily meal allowance.
- Alcohol is never reimbursable.
- Certification exam fees are reimbursed in full on a passing result and for one retake per exam; this reimbursement is separate from, and combines with, the certification bonus.

## Travel

- Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy; flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy.
- Hotel nights are capped at USD 210, or USD 290 in New York, San Francisco, London and Sydney.
- Flights and hotels should be booked 14 days or more before departure where the client schedule allows.
- A rental car is allowed when no public transit connects the airport to the client site; use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at USD 0.67 per mile.
- Mileage reimbursement cannot be combined with rental car or fuel claims on the same trip.
- Non-billable travel of more than 3 hours on a weekend earns a half day of time off in lieu, to be taken within 60 days.
- Travel billed to a client follows the client's travel policy where that policy is stricter than this one; the stricter rule applies and the two do not combine.

## Remote Work

- Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week; during client-site weeks the client's schedule takes precedence.
- Fully remote arrangements need director approval and are reviewed every 6 months.
- Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.
- A home-office stipend of USD 600 is paid once, when the employee joins.
- An internet allowance of USD 45 per month is paid to employees working remotely; it combines with the home-office stipend because they are separate budgets.
- Coworking memberships are reimbursed up to USD 180 per month, but in any month where coworking is claimed the internet allowance is not paid, so the two do not combine.
- Working from abroad is permitted for up to 20 days per calendar year in a country without a company office, with HR and security approval requested 10 business days or more in advance.
- Client data must never be accessed over public wi-fi, wherever the employee is working.

## Certification Bonus

| Tier | Bonus | Credentials |
|---|---|---|
| Associate | USD 200 | Each Salesforce Certified Associate or Salesforce Certified AI Associate credential |
| Administrator | USD 350 | Each Administrator-level credential, including Salesforce Certified Administrator, Advanced Administrator and Marketing Cloud Administrator |
| Specialist | USD 400 | Each Salesforce credential named Specialist, such as Agentforce Specialist and Marketing Cloud Email Specialist; Tableau Desktop Specialist belongs to the Tableau tier instead |
| Consultant | USD 500 | Each Salesforce Certified Consultant credential, such as the Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, Data Cloud, Revenue Cloud and Marketing Cloud Consultant credentials |
| Developer | USD 600 | Each Salesforce Certified Platform Developer credential (I or II); MuleSoft Certified Developer belongs to the MuleSoft tier instead |
| Architect | USD 900 | Each Salesforce Certified Architect credential, such as Application, Data, Integration, and Identity and Access Management Architect; Salesforce Certified Technical Architect is paid USD 2,400 and is not also paid at the Architect tier, and MuleSoft architect credentials belong to the MuleSoft tier |
| Tableau | USD 450 | Each Tableau credential of any level, including Tableau Certified Data Analyst and Tableau Desktop Specialist |
| MuleSoft | USD 550 | Each MuleSoft credential, including MuleSoft Certified Developer, MuleSoft Certified Platform Architect and MuleSoft Certified Integration Architect |

Each certification is paid once, in the payroll run after the certificate date; bonuses for several held certifications stack, and no bonus is paid again for a renewal or retake.

## On-Call

- On-call rotations run for one week, from Monday 09:00 to the following Monday 09:00 in the on-call engineer's local time.
- A weekly on-call allowance of USD 320 is paid for each rotation week served.
- Priority 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 30 minutes and Priority 2 incidents within 2 hours.
- A call-out payment of USD 60 is paid for each Priority 1 incident handled between 18:00 and 08:00 or on a weekend; it combines with the weekly allowance.
- During a client go-live week, a go-live support premium of USD 150 per day replaces the call-out payment for that day; the premium combines with the weekly allowance but not with the call-out payment.
- An employee who handles an incident between 22:00 and 06:00 may start work 11 hours after the incident closes without any loss of pay.
- No employee is placed on call in two consecutive weeks, and rotation swaps need manager approval 48 hours before the rotation starts.

## Data Security

- Client data must stay inside the client's Salesforce org, an approved sandbox or the firm's encrypted project drive, and must never be stored on personal devices.
- All laptops use full-disk encryption, and multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every work account.
- Production data copied into a sandbox must be masked before any developer or tester can access the sandbox.
- Use of the firm-approved password manager is mandatory; passwords are never shared in chat or email.
- A lost or stolen device must be reported to the security lead within 2 hours.
- Access to a client org is removed within 1 business day of the project end date or of an employee's departure.
- Security awareness training must be completed within 30 days of joining and every 12 months afterwards.
- A suspected data breach is reported to the security lead within 4 hours and the client is notified within 48 hours of confirmation; if the client contract sets a shorter notice period, the shorter period applies instead.
